Barometer+ by Tenebras reads atmospheric pressure from the watch’s built-in sensor. Pressure readings and timestamps are used to display trends and charts. Readings, timestamps, and app preferences are stored locally on the watch. Barometer+ does not send these data to the phone, the developer, or external servers.

The About screen displays an optional QR code for the developer’s Telegram channel. If you scan it with another device and open the link, Telegram’s privacy policy applies to that visit.
